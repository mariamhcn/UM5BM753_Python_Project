Biopsy (6.5% positive) is the likely primary target. It's the histological test — a tissue sample read under a microscope — while Hinselmann, Schiller and Citology are the screening tests that a biopsy confirms or rules out. Dx:Cancer was considered and set aside: it's a self-reported prior diagnosis from the intake questionnaire, not an outcome of this screening visit, and at 18 positives (2.1%) it's even sparser than Biopsy. All four sit well under 10% positive: class imbalance needs handling (stratified split, SMOTE on the training fold only) before modeling.

Outliers worth a second look

IQR method (1.5×IQR beyond Q1/Q3), continuous columns only, computed on the typed raw data (before the column drops above). Read with a caveat: most of this dataset's "continuous" variables are zero-inflated (0 for the majority, a real tail for the minority who smoke / used an IUD / had an STD), so a high outlier rate here usually marks a genuine subgroup, not bad data.
